01

THE BEGINNING

Starting at age 10

I began my shooting journey at the age of 10 with 10-metre air rifle shooting. At that stage, I competed in the seated position and gradually developed my focus, discipline, and control.

02

EARLY ACHIEVEMENTS

State medals in seated air rifle

During my seated 10-metre air rifle journey, I earned several state-level medals. These early achievements gave me valuable competition experience and strengthened my foundation as a shooter.

03

A NEW DIRECTION

Finding my event in trap shooting

I later explored standing rifle shooting, but I found that it was not the right fit for me. I decided to pursue trap shooting, a discipline that inspired a new interest and a fresh challenge.

04

2025 · NATIONAL STAGE

Qualifying for Nationals in my first year

In 2025, I qualified for the National Championship in trap shooting for the first time. I recorded a score of 89 out of 125 targets (“birds”), gaining an important and memorable national-level competition experience. I also qualified for international trials.

ATHLETE PROFILE

Ujjwal Tomar

National-level trap shooter · India

Shooting has taught me patience, persistence, and the value of learning from every competition.

I am Ujjwal Tomar, a national-level trap shooter from India. My journey began with seated 10-metre air rifle shooting when I was 10 years old. After earning several state-level medals, I explored different disciplines before discovering my interest in trap shooting.

Qualifying for the 2025 Nationals in my first year of trap shooting was a meaningful milestone. I approach each competition as an opportunity to gain experience, improve my performance, and keep progressing in the sport.
